The Guarantee

I have enough confidence in this process to offer a full refund at the end of the course if you feel you gave it your all, and it hasn't changed you. After the commencement of week 2, the refund will only be given after the full completion of the 14 week programme

The Research

I am conducting ongoing research, collecting anonymous data before, during, and after the programme, with your consent, with the goal of not only improving what I offer, but building a scientifically validated intervention that can one day be applied more broadly, and perhaps even help future men face their own dragons a little better armed

This is not therapy, nor is it an easy and quick fix. It is a structured passage, from where you are, to who you are capable of becoming.
