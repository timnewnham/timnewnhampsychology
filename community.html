The Fellowship

Join a community of like-minded fellows

You can't do it alone, and nor should you want to.

Completing the programme is not the end of your journey, for it opens the door into something more enduring. Those who successfully complete the initiation programme are welcomed as members into an active community of men that uphold the same value system.

In person

The Weekly Challenge

For those local to the area, a recurring weekly physical challenge. These challenges are focused on building resilience, grit, strength, endurance and teamwork. Details on time and place shared with participants directly.

In person

The Monthly Gathering and Yearly Adventure

For those local to the area, a recurring monthly in-person meetup. The meetups will include activities such as discussions on topics of personal growth, book readings and essay writing, skills development, community volunteer work, hobbies and activities, BBQs, and most importantly the support of each other at times of need. Additionally, once a year we will come together for an adventurous multi-day trip somewhere in Europe. Details on time and place shared with participants directly.

Online

Community Discussion Forums

An online space for past and current participants to stay connected with each other, to share what they're working through, and to keep each other on track and supported long after the formal programme ends.

This community is currently being established alongside the first cohorts of the programme itself. Early participants won't just be joining The Fellowship, they'll be helping shape it's future.
