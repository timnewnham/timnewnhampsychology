He who guides thee

I have walked this path.

I'm Tim Newnham, an Australian now living in Groningen, a former Australian infantry soldier, health coach, and psychology student at the University of Groningen. My own journey from struggle to purpose wasn't clean or linear. It required a lot of exploration and research to incorporate my whole self.

My experiences, combined with formal training in psychology, are what I bring to this work.

The stones

Placed by those before us to guide us over the pass

The cairn is an ancient symbol, each stone placed by those who came before, marking the way for those who follow. This programme draws on the same principle: a foundation of proven frameworks, combined in layers with intention.

Psychological theory, Stoic philosophy, anthropological research, knowledge from subject matter experts. Nothing invented. Nothing trendy. Everything grounded and tested.
