Who This Is For

You know something has to change.

Whether you're 15 or 30, there's a version of yourself you haven't become yet. Not because you lack ability — but because no one has shown you the path, or held you accountable to walking it.

This programme is for young men who are lost, stuck, or quietly struggling. Who sense there is more to who they could be, but don't know how to get there.

Who I Am

I have walked this path.

I'm Tim Newnham — Australian, former infantry soldier, fitness coach, and psychology student at the University of Groningen. My own road from disarray to direction wasn't clean or linear. It required people who refused to let me stay where I was.

That experience — combined with formal training in psychology — is what I bring to this work.

The Methods

Built on what works.

The cairn is an ancient symbol — each stone placed by those who came before, marking the way for those who follow. This programme draws on the same principle: a foundation of proven frameworks, layered with intention.

Jungian psychology, Stoic philosophy, the Hero's Journey, Self-Determination Theory, DBT, and the initiation structures of Van Gennep. Nothing invented. Everything tested.
